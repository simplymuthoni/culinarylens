# **App Name**: Culinary Lens

## Core Features:

- Image Upload: Allow users to upload photos of meals.
- AI Food Identification: Utilize AI to identify the dish from the uploaded photo. Use image recognition to discern ingredients and culinary style.
- Recipe Generation: Instantly generate a detailed recipe for the identified dish using a generative AI tool. The AI decides whether a listed ingredient may be omitted without significant effect.
- Recipe Display: Display the complete recipe with a list of ingredients, step-by-step instructions, and nutritional information.
- AI-Generated Dish Image: Generate an image of the dish based on the identified food, ingredients and recipe details.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Earthy green (#849B87) to evoke nature and freshness.
- Background color: Warm cream (#F5F5DC) to provide a comforting and appetizing backdrop. It is a light color scheme.
- Accent color: Muted olive (#A2A98F) to complement the primary and create depth.
- Body and headline font: 'Alegreya', a humanist serif with an elegant and intellectual feel.
- Use simple, line-style icons representing ingredients and cooking steps.
- Clean and spacious layout with a focus on the food images and recipe details.
